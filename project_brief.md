# Project Brief

## Project Title

Communities Near the Benue River in Makurdi, Nigeria

## Spatial Question

Which communities in Makurdi LGA, Benue State, are located within 1 km of the Benue River?

## Study Area

Makurdi Local Government Area (LGA), Benue State, Nigeria.

## Project Overview

This project will use Geographic Information Systems (GIS) to identify communities in Makurdi LGA that are located within 1 km of the Benue River.

A 1 km buffer will be created around the Benue River, and settlement locations will be analysed to identify communities that fall within the buffer.

## Data Required

### 1. Makurdi LGA Administrative Boundary

**Purpose:** Define the study area and limit the analysis to Makurdi LGA.

**Source:** GADM

**Source Link:** https://gadm.org/data.html

### 2. Benue River / Waterways

**Purpose:** Identify and map the Benue River within the study area.

**Source:** OpenStreetMap

**Source Link:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/

### 3. Settlement / Community Locations

**Purpose:** Identify communities in Makurdi LGA and determine which are located within 1 km of the Benue River.

**Source:** OpenStreetMap

**Source Link:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/

## Planned GIS Analysis

1. Obtain the Makurdi LGA administrative boundary.
2. Obtain the Benue River data.
3. Obtain settlement/community locations.
4. Create a 1 km buffer around the Benue River.
5. Identify communities that fall within the 1 km buffer.
6. Produce a map showing the river, buffer, and communities within the buffer.

## Expected Output

A GIS map showing communities in Makurdi LGA that are located within 1 km of the Benue River.

## Project Scope

This project focuses on spatial proximity between communities and the Benue River.

Being located within 1 km of the river will be treated as a proximity measure and will not by itself be interpreted as evidence of flood risk or environmental risk.
